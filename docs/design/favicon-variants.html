Favicon variants — Fiore Vera

The family crest (“címer”) direction for the tab icon: the badge’s open arc with its trailing dots, always carrying a letter. Each variant is shown as inline SVG at 16 / 32 / 48 / 180 px on a light and a dark browser-tab strip, plus the real 16 px pixels as rasterised by sharp. Palette limited to the locked tokens; letterforms are Cormorant Garamond converted to paths — no live font.

Finding while auditing the current set (2026-11-09). public/favicon.svg was a generic daisy, while every PNG (favicon-16x16, -32x32, apple-touch-icon, android-chrome-*) was the full line-art badge squeezed into a square — unreadable at 16 and 32 px. Chrome/Firefox showed the daisy, Safari and home-screen bookmarks the smudged badge. npm run favicon now regenerates all of them from one SVG so this cannot drift again.

Recommendation

E (the crest), with A (the monogram) as runner-up, for a pure 16 px tab icon. F (A + E combined), added on request, is the best choice if the 180 px and larger sizes matter more than the 16 px tab — see its critique. For a more colourful F, F1 (blush tile, sage arc, gold dots) is my pick: it uses three brand tints without reading as a rainbow; F2 is the same tile with a sturdier ink arc for 16 px; F3 and F4 are the sage and ink grounds. Two further colourways (gold arc or sage arc on plain linen) were tested and dropped: their accents vanish at 16 px on a light tab strip.

The treble-clef F (G1–G3) answers the owner’s crest brief: a family “címer” that always carries a letter. G2 is the version where the F stays a letter at 16 px; G3 is G2 in the F1 colourway and is what I would ship if the clef direction wins. Honest reservation: a violin key is a strong statement — it says music before it says flowers, and nothing on the site explains it. If there is a music story behind Fiore Vera, it is the most personal mark of the whole round; if there is not, F1 is the safer crest.

Decision (2026-11-09): the owner picked F3 (crest-fv-sage), now shipped.
